# Project support

Report reproducible project bugs through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/fayselomerahmed/deutsch-b1-coach-ios/issues). Include the app version, iOS version, expected behavior, and steps to reproduce. Issues are public: never include personal information, passwords, Access IDs, purchase receipts, or voice recordings.

For purchase refunds and billing questions, use Apple's purchase support. A production distributor must provide a private support and privacy-request channel before release.
