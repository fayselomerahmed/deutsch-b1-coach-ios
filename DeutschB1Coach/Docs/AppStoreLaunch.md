# App Store Launch Package

## Listing

**Name:** Deutsch B1 Coach

**Subtitle:** German for life and B1

**Positioning line:** Pass B1. Handle real life in Germany. Learn in your language.

**Promotional text:** Learn German visually, practise corrected real-life conversations with private natural audio, and follow a clear A1-B1 path with orientation and B1 readiness practice.

**Keywords:** deutsch lernen,B1,DTZ,Integrationskurs,Leben in Deutschland,sprechen,Grammatik,Wortschatz

The description must state that exam and orientation material is independent practice, not an official endorsement or pass guarantee.

## Screenshot Story

1. German for life in Germany: show the next lesson and daily path.
2. Practise real conversations: show transcript, correction, and retry audio.
3. See and remember every word: show the large image, article, singular, plural, and sentence.
4. Understand grammar visually: show sentence structure and the Grammar X-Ray.
5. Prepare all four B1 skills: show readiness sections and next-focus guidance.
6. Learn in your language: show Amharic, Tigrinya, Arabic, and other teaching-language choices.
7. Prepare for Leben in Deutschland: show four-choice questions and the randomized mock test.

Screenshots must show the working app, large readable text, no prices, and no unverifiable superlatives.

## Audience Pages

Create custom product pages with matching screenshots and campaign links for:

- B1 and DTZ preparation.
- German for Amharic speakers.
- German for Tigrinya speakers.
- Life and work in Germany.
- Orientation and Leben in Deutschland preparation.

## Launch Offer

- Monthly: 9.99 EUR.
- Annual: 59.99 EUR and seven-day introductory trial.
- Founding annual offer: 39.99 EUR for the first 500 redemptions through partner-distributed Apple offer codes.
- No lifetime product at launch.

## Ninety-Day Rollout

Weeks 1-3: complete teacher review and observed learner sessions; repair every accuracy, crash, and comprehension blocker.

Weeks 4-6: run TestFlight with partner schools and community groups; verify the StoreKit sandbox, trial wording, restoration, support, privacy, and review notes.

Weeks 7-12: launch audience-specific pages and offer codes with integration schools, migrant-support organizations, employers, and Amharic/Tigrinya community educators. Improve the weakest funnel step weekly; do not add unrelated features.

## Operating Targets

- First lesson completion: at least 60%.
- Day-seven retention: at least 20%.
- Trial-to-paid conversion: at least 35%.
- Refund rate: below 5%.
- Annual plan share: above 60% of new paid subscriptions.

The app records only aggregate milestones locally and never includes names, answers, or transcripts. Use App Store Connect for acquisition, proceeds, retention, and product-page comparisons. Any future app-owned telemetry must be strictly opt-in and documented before release.
