# Teacher And Learner Release Review

Paid acquisition is blocked until this review is complete. Automated integrity tests protect structure; qualified people must approve teaching quality, cultural clarity, visual accuracy, and real-world usefulness.

## Review Group

- Three to five qualified German teachers, including at least one teacher experienced with integration courses or DTZ preparation.
- Thirty to fifty learners across A1, A2, and B1.
- At least five learners using Amharic or Tigrinya as the teaching language.

## Teacher Sign-Off

Each reviewer completes a representative journey at every level and records pass, revision, or blocker for:

- CEFR-appropriate vocabulary, grammar sequence, and cognitive load.
- Natural, non-repetitive German examples from daily life, work, housing, health, shopping, and public services.
- Correct articles, plurals, cases, word order, translations, and pronunciation.
- Accurate vocabulary images that show the exact word rather than a related object.
- Speaking corrections that preserve the learner's intended meaning and never claim certainty beyond the local scoring method.
- Original B1 practice that reflects listening, reading, writing, and speaking task demands without claiming official endorsement.
- Orientation material checked against the current official BAMF catalogue before each release.

No blocker may remain. Revision findings need a second-person check before publication.

## Learner Sessions

Observe learners without coaching them through the interface. Measure whether they can:

- Choose a teaching language and complete placement.
- Start the correct level and finish the first lesson.
- Understand a visual vocabulary card and play singular, plural, and example audio.
- Complete a speaking sample, understand the correction, and retry.
- Find orientation questions and start a randomized mock test.
- Explain what the paid plan provides, its duration, and how to cancel.

Record completion time, confusion points, crashes, abandoned screens, and the learner's own description of value. Do not record voice, identity, or lesson answers without explicit consent.

## Release Decision

Release only when all crash blockers are closed, at least 80% of test learners complete the first lesson without help, every tested image and translation is accurate, and all subscription wording matches App Store Connect.
