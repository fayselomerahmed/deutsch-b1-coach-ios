# Product And Monetization

Deutsch B1 Coach is the integration and B1 readiness coach for newcomers building an independent life in Germany.

Primary promise: **Pass B1. Handle real life in Germany. Learn in your language.**

The product should not compete as a generic language library. Its defensible combination is multilingual teaching, visual memory, private natural voice, corrected real-life speaking, chronological A1-B1 lessons, orientation questions, and a four-skill B1 readiness plan.

## Revenue Model

The launch offer is a two-plan StoreKit 2 subscription:

- 9.99 EUR monthly.
- 59.99 EUR annually, shown first as the best-value plan.
- Seven-day introductory trial for eligible new annual subscribers, configured in App Store Connect.
- A limited 39.99 EUR founding annual offer for the first partner cohorts, distributed with Apple offer codes.

Do not offer lifetime access at launch. Existing lifetime entitlements remain restorable for compatibility, but the product is hidden from the purchase screen because curriculum review, live updates, support, and speech quality create continuing costs.

The free experience includes placement, orientation questions, A1 lessons 1-5, the A1 vocabulary and grammar libraries, the repeatable A1 daily practice loop, the review queue, and basic progress. A learner placed at A2 or B1 also receives the first lesson at that level, one corrected speaking sample, and one eight-question B1 readiness direction before the upgrade decision.

The full version unlocks A1 lesson 6 onward, every A2 and B1 lesson and library, advanced skill practice, real-life roleplays and the phrasebook, learning games, B1 exam preparation, and detailed progress insights. Consumer access is unlocked with a verified App Store purchase. Managed access is presented separately as school or partner access for institutional agreements. Study history remains on-device and visible when access expires.

## Conversion Principles

- Let learners experience a complete lesson, correction, or readiness direction before showing an upgrade.
- Present the annual plan clearly without artificial countdowns or deceptive urgency.
- Keep study history accessible after an entitlement expires.
- Explain subscription duration, renewal, restoration, and privacy in plain language.
- Describe local heuristic feedback as an on-device coach, not as generative AI.

## Release Requirements

- Configure monthly and annual product identifiers, prices, localization, and review screenshots in App Store Connect.
- Configure the seven-day introductory trial and founding offer codes in App Store Connect.
- Publish support, privacy-policy, and terms URLs.
- Validate purchases in an Apple sandbox account and verify restoration across devices.
- Complete the teacher and learner quality review before paid acquisition.

## Distribution

Use two channels from launch:

- Consumer annual subscriptions through the App Store.
- Institutional access for integration schools, employers, municipalities, NGOs, and language teachers through privacy-preserving Access IDs.

Create separate App Store custom product pages for B1/DTZ preparation, German for Amharic speakers, German for Tigrinya speakers, and life and work in Germany. Partner campaigns should use offer codes and the matching custom page.

Do not buy broad paid traffic until the first-lesson completion, day-seven retention, and trial conversion targets are met. See `AppStoreLaunch.md` for the launch package and operating targets.
